About Smart Funds USA

Smart Funds USA helps everyday borrowers compare personal loan options in one place — without the runaround of applying to lender after lender.

Who we are

Smart Funds USA is a personal-loan matching service based in Las Vegas, Nevada. Our job is simple: take one short request and connect it to lenders and lending partners who may be able to help with a personal loan between $100 and $5,000. We built Smart Funds USA for people who need a straightforward answer fast — not another maze of applications.

What we do — and don't do

  • We do operate a free service that matches your request with lenders in our network.
  • We do use encryption to protect the information you share with us in transit.
  • We do not lend money, make credit decisions, or set the rate, fee, or term of any loan.
  • We do not charge you to use the service — Smart Funds USA is always free to consumers.

Plain and simple: Smart Funds USA is a connector, not a lender. The lender you are matched with writes the offer, sets the rate, and funds the loan.

How we earn

When we connect you with a lender or lending partner, that lender may compensate Smart Funds USA for the referral. This is a common model in the lending marketplace and it is how we keep the service free for you. Compensation never changes what you pay on a loan and never adds a fee from Smart Funds USA. For the full picture, see our Advertising Disclosure.

Our commitment to you

We believe borrowing decisions deserve clarity. That means telling you exactly what Smart Funds USA is, exactly how we are paid, and exactly what to expect. It means encrypting your data, giving you a real way to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information, and making it easy to unsubscribe at any time. You can always reach a person on our Contact page.
