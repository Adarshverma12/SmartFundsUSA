Personal Loan Questions, Answered

Straight answers on speed, cost, credit, safety, and how our free connecting service actually works — before you request a thing.

The 10 things people ask us most

Below are the questions we hear again and again. If yours isn't here, our Contact page is the fastest way to reach a real person. Remember: Smart Funds USA is a connecting service, not a lender, and requesting your options is always free.

How fast can I get the money?

If a lender in the network approves your request and you e-sign their agreement, funds are often deposited as soon as the next business day.* The exact timing depends on when you finish, which lender you connect with, and how quickly your own bank posts the deposit. Weekends and bank holidays can push things back, so requesting earlier in the day tends to move faster.

Can I request a personal loan with bad credit?

Yes — you're welcome to submit a request no matter what your credit looks like. Many lenders in the network weigh your current income and ability to repay alongside your credit history, so a low score doesn't automatically rule you out. Approval is never guaranteed, and each lender sets its own criteria, but all credit types are welcome to apply.

How much does it cost to use Smart Funds USA?

Requesting your loan options through Smart Funds USA is $0 — we never charge you a fee to submit a request or to see whether a lender wants to work with you. We're a free connecting service, not a lender, and we may receive compensation from lenders when we make a connection. Any interest, fees, or charges on an actual loan come from the lender you choose and are spelled out in their agreement before you sign.

Is my information safe?

Protecting your details is a priority. Information you send through our site is encrypted in transit with 256-bit SSL and stored using AES-256 encryption, so it can't be read as plain text if intercepted. We only share what's needed to connect you with lenders and lending partners in the network — see our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How much can I request?

You can request a personal loan from $100 up to $5,000 through Smart Funds USA. The amount you're actually offered depends on the lender, your state, and your financial profile — a lender may approve less than you asked for. Borrow only what you can comfortably repay, even if you're offered more.

How do I repay the loan?

You repay the lender directly, not Smart Funds USA, since we aren't the one funding your loan. Repayment terms, due dates, and payment methods are set by the lender and laid out in the loan agreement you review before signing. Many lenders use automatic withdrawals from your bank account, so read the terms closely and reach out to your lender if you ever expect a payment to be tight.

Is Smart Funds USA available in my state?

This service isn't available in every state, because lending rules and available lenders differ from one state to the next. The quickest way to find out is to start a request — if no lenders serve your area, you'll know right away and it costs you nothing. Loan amounts, rates, and terms also vary by state and by lender.

How does Smart Funds USA make money?

We're free for you to use, so we earn through the lenders instead — we may receive compensation from a lender or lending partner when we connect you and you move forward. That compensation doesn't add a fee to your loan or change the terms a lender offers you. Our role is simply to match your request with lenders in the network; we don't make loans or approval decisions.

Will requesting affect my credit score?

Submitting a request through Smart Funds USA to see your options doesn't hurt your credit. However, if you continue with a lender, that lender may verify your details through credit bureaus or alternative data providers, which can involve a credit inquiry. Once you have a loan, keep in mind that paying late or missing a payment can lower your credit score.

What if no lender approves my request?

A request is never a guarantee that a lender will accept it or make an offer, and that's okay — you owe nothing and can try again later. If no lender in our network is a fit, you may still be shown other offers or options from third parties. It's always worth reviewing your budget and only borrowing what you can repay before you request again.

Some lenders in the network may be tribal lenders operating under tribal law, and their rates and fees may be higher than state-licensed lenders. Any dollar or APR figures shown across this site are illustrative. See Rates & Fees for representative examples.
