How fast can I get the money?
SF

If a lender approves your request and you accept the offer, funds are typically sent by direct deposit as soon as the next business day. Exact timing depends on the lender and your bank.

Can I request a personal loan with bad credit?
SF

Yes. All credit types are welcome to submit a request. Lenders in the network look at more than a credit score, including income and employment. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

How much does it cost to use Smart Funds USA?
SF

Nothing. Smart Funds USA is free for consumers. We are not a lender — we connect your request with lenders and lending partners who may pay us for that connection. Your lender will disclose all loan costs before you sign.

Is my information safe?
SF

Your request is protected with 256-bit SSL encryption in transit and stored with industry-standard AES-256 encryption. We only share your information as described in our Privacy Policy.

How much can I request?

You can request between $100 and $5,000. The amount you are offered depends on the lender, your state, and your financial profile.
