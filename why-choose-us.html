Why Choose Smart Funds USA

There are plenty of ways to look for a loan. Here is why starting with Smart Funds USA saves you time — and keeps you in control.

One request instead of ten applications

Applying to lenders one at a time is slow and repetitive. With Smart Funds USA you complete a single short request and let interested lenders in the network respond. You compare what comes back rather than starting over each time.

All credit types are welcome

Good credit, fair credit, or a score you are still rebuilding — you are welcome to submit a request. Many lenders in the network consider more than a credit score, including income and employment. Submitting a request never guarantees an offer, but it costs nothing to see what is available.

Free, with no obligation

Smart Funds USA is always free to consumers, and you are never obligated to accept an offer. If nothing fits, you walk away — no cost, no pressure.

Built with security in mind

The information you submit is protected with 256-bit SSL encryption in transit and handled according to our Privacy Policy. You control your data: you can opt out of its sale or sharing and unsubscribe from messages whenever you want.

Straight answers, in plain English

We tell you what we are (a matching service), what we are not (a lender), and exactly how we are paid on our Advertising Disclosure page. If a term is confusing, our Definitions glossary breaks it down.

Remember: Smart Funds USA does not make loan decisions or set rates. Every offer, APR, and fee is set by the lender. Always read the lender's terms before you sign, and only accept a loan you can comfortably repay.
