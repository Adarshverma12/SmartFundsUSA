Talk to a Human at Smart Funds USA

Real questions get real answers. Whether you're wondering how the connecting service works, what happens to your information, or how to opt out — a person on our side reads it and replies. Just know one thing up front: anything about an active loan belongs with your lender, not with us.

Where your message should go

Two inboxes, one simple rule

Smart Funds USA is a free service that connects you with a network of lenders and lending partners — we are not a lender ourselves. That shapes who can help you fastest. Questions about this website, your data, unsubscribing, or how the service works come straight to us by email. Questions about an offer, your balance, a payment date, or a payoff are things only your lender can see and change, so those go directly to them. Sorting it up front saves you a round trip.

Email us
