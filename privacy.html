Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in plain English, what information Smart Funds USA collects when you use our free connecting service, how we use it, who we share it with, and the privacy rights you have — including your California rights and how to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

Effective date: July 1, 2026

Overview

Smart Funds USA (“Smart Funds USA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website at smartfundsusa.com (the “Site”). We are a free service that connects consumers who are looking for a personal loan of $100–$5,000 with a network of third-party lenders and lending partners. We are not a lender, we do not make credit decisions, and we do not fund loans.

Because our core function is to route your request to lenders who may be able to help, sharing your information with those lenders and lending partners is the central purpose of this Site. This policy describes how that works so you can decide what you are comfortable submitting. By using the Site or submitting a request, you agree to the practices described here.

This policy applies only to information collected through the Site. It does not cover the privacy practices of any lender, lending partner, service provider, or other third party that may receive your information — each of those has its own privacy policy that governs how it treats your data.

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Identifiers you provide. When you fill out a loan request or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, mailing/home address, date of birth, and Social Security number.
  • Financial information you enter. To match you with lenders, our request form asks for details such as your desired loan amount, income and pay frequency, employment information, and bank account details (for example, routing and account information used by lenders for verification and, if you are approved and accept an offer, for deposit and repayment).
  • Device and usage data. We and our providers automatically collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring/exit pages, the pages you view on the Site, and the dates and times of your visits.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember preferences, measure traffic, and support marketing. See Cookies & tracking below.

You choose what to submit. Some fields are required to process a loan request, and if you do not provide them, we may be unable to connect you with lenders.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your request and connect you with lenders and lending partners in our network who may be able to offer you a loan;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site and our service;
  • Respond to your questions, requests, and support inquiries;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and other unlawful or unauthorized activity;
  • Measure and analyze how the Site is used so we can improve it;
  • Send you service-related or, where permitted, marketing communications (you can opt out of marketing at any time); and
  • Comply with applicable laws and enforce our Terms of Use and other agreements.

How we share information

We share your information in the following ways:

  • With lenders and lending partners — to fulfill your request. This is the key point: when you submit a loan request through the Site, we share the information you enter with one or more third-party lenders, lending partners, and marketing partners in our network so they can evaluate your request and, if interested, present you with offers. Once your information is shared, each lender or partner uses it under its own privacy policy and terms, which we do not control.
  • With service providers. We share information with vendors that perform services on our behalf — such as hosting, data storage, analytics, fraud prevention, identity verification, and communications — under agreements that limit their use of the data to those services.
  • For legal reasons. We may disclose information when we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or lawful government request; to enforce our terms; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Smart Funds USA, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or bankruptcy, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not control, and are not responsible for, how lenders, lending partners, or other third parties use your information after we share it. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before accepting any offer.

Cookies & tracking

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Site working, remember your preferences, understand how the Site is used, and support advertising and measurement. Some cookies are set by third parties, such as analytics and advertising providers.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them, and you may be able to use industry opt-out tools for interest-based advertising. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect how the Site functions. Because there is no consistent industry standard, we do not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals; however, we do treat recognized opt-out preference signals as described in the California section below.

Your choices & rights

  • Marketing communications. You can opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in those messages or by emailing us. You may still receive non-marketing, service-related messages.
  • Cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser and available opt-out tools.
  • Access and updates. You may contact us to ask about the information we hold or to request corrections.
  • State privacy rights. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights. California residents should see the section below.

Your California Privacy Rights & “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information”

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“CCPA/CPRA”), gives you specific rights regarding your personal information. Subject to certain exceptions and verification, you have the right to:

  • Right to know. Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources of that information, the business or commercial purpose for collecting or sharing it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to delete. Request that we delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct. Request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we share the information you submit with lenders, lending partners, and marketing partners — which may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under the CCPA/CPRA — you have the right to opt out of that sale or sharing.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. You may direct us to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information (such as your Social Security number and financial account information) to what is necessary to perform the service you requested. To exercise this right, email us using the instructions below.

How to submit a request. To exercise any of these rights, including to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, email us at info@smartfundsusa.com with the subject line “California Privacy Request” and tell us which right you want to exercise. We will need to verify your identity before acting on requests to know, delete, or correct. You may also submit a request through an authorized agent, and we will take reasonable steps to verify the agent’s authority. We honor recognized opt-out preference signals, such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC), where required.

Note that opting out of the sale or sharing of your personal information means we will no longer route new requests to lenders on your behalf, which may prevent us from matching you with a loan offer — that is the core service the Site provides.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it. Retention periods vary based on the type of data and the reason we hold it.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including 256-bit SSL encryption for data in transit and AES-256 encryption for sensitive data. While we work hard to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping any account or contact information you use with us confidential.

Children’s privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Third-party links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites, including lenders, lending partners, and other resources. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of those sites. Once you leave the Site or your information is shared with a third party, this policy no longer applies, and you should review that third party’s own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes may be communicated through the Site or by other reasonable means. Your continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
