Advertising Disclosure

Straight talk on how a free service like ours keeps the lights on — who pays us, what that changes, and what it never changes for you.

Updated July 2026

We think you deserve to know how the sites you use actually make money. Smart Funds USA is a free service for consumers — you will never pay us a fee to request a personal loan of $100–$5,000, and we don't take a cut out of any funds you receive. This page explains where our revenue really comes from and, just as importantly, where it doesn't.

We're free for you — always

It costs you $0 to use Smart Funds USA. Filling out our request form, getting connected with lenders in our network, and reviewing any offers you're presented with are all free. We do not charge application fees, membership fees, or "processing" fees, and we will never ask you to pay us anything to be matched with a lender.

How we actually earn

When we connect you with a lender or lending partner in our network, that lender or partner may pay us compensation for the referral. In plain terms: the businesses on the receiving end of your request — not you — are the ones who cover our costs. This is a common arrangement for loan-connecting services and is the reason we can keep the service free for the people we're trying to help.

Compensation may take the form of a flat fee, a per-lead fee, or a revenue-share arrangement, and it can vary from one lender or partner to the next. We may also earn from advertising or affiliate relationships displayed on the site.

What that compensation can influence

Because different lenders and partners may compensate us at different rates, that compensation may influence which lenders or offers appear on our site and the order in which they're shown. A lender paying us more may be presented more prominently than one paying us less. We want to be upfront about that, because "sort order" and "who shows up first" are exactly the kinds of things a disclosure like this exists to reveal.

What it never changes

Here's the part that matters most: the compensation we receive does not change the price you're quoted. The lender sets your rate, fees, and terms based on your financial profile, your state, and the lender's own criteria — not based on what they pay us. Our getting compensated doesn't add a markup to your loan or raise your cost of borrowing.

We are not a lender, and we don't endorse anyone

Smart Funds USA is not a lender. We don't make loans, we don't broker loans, and we don't make loan or credit decisions. We also don't endorse, recommend, or vouch for any specific lender in our network. Being in our network — or appearing prominently on our site — is not a stamp of approval from us and shouldn't be read as one.

Our goal is simply to connect you with options. Whether any option is right for you is a decision only you can make — so before you accept anything, read the lender's full terms carefully, including the APR, total repayment amount, fees, and payment schedule. Any dollar figures or APRs you see on our site are illustrative only.

Before you accept an offer

Being connected with a lender is the beginning of the conversation, not the end. We strongly encourage you to:

  • Compare the full terms of each offer side by side, not just the monthly payment.
  • Check the APR and the total cost of the loan over its full term.
  • Confirm the lender is licensed to operate where you live; some lenders in the network may be tribal lenders operating under tribal law, and their rates and fees may be higher than state-licensed lenders.
  • Borrow only what you can realistically repay on time.

Questions?

If anything here is unclear or you'd like to know more about how we operate, we're happy to explain. Contact us and we'll get back to you.
