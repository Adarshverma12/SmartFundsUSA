How Personal Loans Work — From Request to Funded

One short request. A whole network of lenders reviewing it. Here's exactly what happens after you hit submit — no jargon, no surprises, no cost to ask.

Start here

What Smart Funds USA actually does

Smart Funds USA is not a lender. We're a free connecting service that takes one request for a $100–$5,000 personal loan and puts it in front of a network of lenders and lending partners at the same time. Instead of filling out a dozen applications on a dozen sites, you tell us what you need once — and we do the legwork of finding lenders who may be able to help.

That single request is the whole idea. You stay in control the entire way: you choose whether to move forward, and you only ever sign with a lender after they've shown you the full terms. We may receive compensation when we connect you with a lender, but that never changes what you pay or which offers you see.

The three steps

From "I need this" to money in your account

1

Ask once

Open the request form and tell us the essentials: how much you want (anywhere from $100 to $5,000), your income, and how to reach you. It takes about 60 seconds and runs over a 256-bit SSL / AES-256 encrypted connection. Nothing to print, mail, or fax.

What to expect: a handful of plain questions, a progress you can see, and no cost to hit submit.

2

Get matched

Your request is shared with lenders in the network for review. This part is usually near-instant — many people see a result within moments. If a lender is interested, you're taken to their offer, where the actual loan terms live.

What to expect: a decision from the lender, not from us — and the freedom to walk away if the offer isn't right.

3

Get funded

Read the lender's terms carefully — the amount, the rate, the fees, and the repayment schedule. If you agree and sign, the lender deposits the funds. Approved loans can arrive as soon as the next business day.*

What to expect: full disclosure before you commit, and money sent straight to your bank account once you say yes.

Eligibility

Do you qualify?

Lenders set their own rules, but most look for a few common basics before they'll review a request. If these describe you, you're generally in a good position to ask:

  • You're at least 18 years old
  • You're a U.S. resident or citizen
  • You have a regular source of income
  • You have an active checking account
  • You have a valid email address and phone number

These are typical requirements set by lenders — not a checklist we control, and not a guarantee. Every lender in the network applies its own criteria, and meeting these basics does not mean you'll be approved.

Behind the decision

What lenders look at

A credit score is only one piece of the picture. Lenders in the network tend to weigh the whole story: how much you earn, whether your income is steady, how long you've been employed, and what your banking history looks like. That's good news if your credit has taken a few hits — it means the number on your report isn't the only thing talking.

All credit types are welcome to submit a request. That said, being welcome to ask is not the same as being approved. Submitting a request is a starting point, not a decision — the lender makes the final call, and they'll tell you exactly where you stand before anything is signed.

Timing

How fast is funding?

The request itself takes about 60 seconds, and matching with a lender is often near-instant. Once you accept an offer and sign, approved loans can be funded as soon as the next business day.* The exact timing isn't something we can promise — it depends on the lender you sign with and how quickly your own bank processes the deposit. Apply earlier in the day, on a business day, and you generally give yourself the best shot at fast funding.

The price of asking

What it costs you

Requesting your options through Smart Funds USA is free — $0 to submit, every time. We don't charge you to connect you with lenders. Any cost you take on comes from the loan itself, and the lender is required to disclose the full amount, rate, fees, and repayment terms before you sign anything. Nothing is hidden, and nothing is owed until you agree to a specific offer.

Loan amounts, APRs, and terms vary by lender, state, and your financial profile, and any figures you see on this site are illustrative. For representative examples of what a loan can cost, see our Loan Options page.

Quick answers

Common questions

Do I need good credit?

No. All credit types are welcome to submit a request, including thin or damaged credit. Lenders in the network look at more than a single score — things like your income and banking history matter too. Submitting a request is not a guarantee of approval, and each lender sets its own criteria.

How long does the form take?

Most people finish the short request in about 60 seconds. You'll enter basic details like the amount you want, your income, and contact and banking information so lenders can review your request. There's nothing to print, mail, or fax.

Will this affect my credit score?

Submitting a request through Smart Funds USA does not affect your credit score. If you move forward with a specific lender, that lender may run a credit check as part of its own review, and any inquiry is disclosed by the lender before you agree to anything.
