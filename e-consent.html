Electronic Communications Consent (E-Consent)

Requesting loan options online means signing and receiving your paperwork on a screen instead of on paper. This consent explains what that involves, what you need to make it work, and how to change your mind.

Effective date: July 1, 2026

By checking the box, clicking a button to submit your request, or otherwise moving forward on smartfundsusa.com, you agree to the terms below. Please read them before you continue, and save or print a copy for your records. In this document, "we," "us," and "our" mean Smart Funds USA; "you" and "your" mean the person requesting loan options. Smart Funds USA is not a lender and does not make credit decisions — we connect you with lenders and lending partners in our network.

Scope of your consent

Under the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (the "ESIGN Act") and comparable state laws, records and disclosures that must normally be delivered on paper may instead be delivered electronically once you agree. By accepting this E-Consent, you consent to:

  • Receive records, notices, agreements, and disclosures from us in electronic form rather than on paper.
  • Use electronic signatures and electronic records to enter into agreements and transactions with us and, where they choose to rely on this consent, with the lenders and lending partners you are matched with.
  • Have your electronic submissions treated as if you had signed and delivered them on paper.

This consent applies to the request you are submitting and to your ongoing relationship with us. Individual lenders may ask you to agree to their own separate electronic-records consent before they extend an offer.

Communications covered

The records and communications you agree to receive electronically may include, but are not limited to:

  • This E-Consent and our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Disclaimer, and Advertising Disclosure, along with any updates to them.
  • Confirmations that your request was received or forwarded to lenders in the network.
  • Notices about your request, your account, or the status of a match.
  • Servicing, marketing, and administrative messages we are permitted to send you.
  • Any disclosure or notice that a law, rule, or regulation requires us to provide.

We may deliver these electronically by posting them on our website, emailing them to the address you provide, or linking to them from an email or web page. It is your responsibility to review each communication we make available.

Hardware & software requirements

To receive, view, and keep electronic records, you confirm that you have access to all of the following:

  • A computer or mobile device with internet access.
  • A current, supported version of a modern web browser (for example, recent releases of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge) with cookies and JavaScript enabled.
  • A valid, active email address and the ability to send and receive email.
  • Software capable of displaying and reading PDF documents.
  • Enough storage, plus a printer or another device, so you can save, download, or print the records we provide for your files.

If our system requirements change in a way that could stop you from accessing or keeping electronic records, we will tell you and, where the law requires, give you the chance to withdraw your consent without penalty.

Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw this consent at any time. To do so, email us at info@smartfundsusa.com with your full name, the email address on file, and a clear statement that you are withdrawing your consent to electronic records.

Withdrawing your consent will not affect the validity of anything you signed or received electronically before the withdrawal took effect. Because our service operates online, withdrawing consent generally means we can no longer process your request or continue connecting you with lenders, and you may need to stop using the service. We will apply your withdrawal within a reasonable time after we receive it. There is no fee to withdraw.

Keeping your contact info current

Electronic delivery only works if we can reach you. You agree to keep the email address and other contact details you gave us accurate and up to date, and to notify us promptly of any change by emailing info@smartfundsusa.com. If email sent to your address is returned as undeliverable, or if you no longer have access to that address, you should update it right away. We are not responsible for records you fail to receive because your contact information was out of date or your inbox blocked or filtered our messages.

Requesting paper copies

Even after you consent to electronic records, you may ask for a paper copy of any record we have provided to you electronically. Send your request to info@smartfundsusa.com, include the record you want and your mailing address, and we will provide it. We do not currently charge a fee for paper copies, though we reserve the right to charge a reasonable fee in the future and will tell you before we do. Asking for a paper copy of a specific record does not, by itself, withdraw your consent to electronic delivery.

Legal effect of electronic signatures

You agree that your electronic signature — including clicking "submit," checking a consent box, or typing your name where requested — has the same legal force and effect as a handwritten signature on paper. Electronic records you accept or we deliver are legally binding, satisfy any legal requirement that a communication be in writing, and can be used as evidence in any proceeding to the same extent as an original paper document. By continuing, you also confirm that you can access and retain this E-Consent in electronic form.
